About Me

Somnath Mali

The Story

Grew up in Karagani, Sangli — a place where the career options were farming or government jobs. I chose a third one nobody in my family understood: coding. Started from home with HTML, then PHP because someone on a forum said it "runs on any server." Three years later, I'm building fintech systems and AI/LLM-powered platforms with Python.

Right now I work at CodeX Technologies in Mumbai. I've built payment systems, wallet platforms, prepaid card integrations, membership ecosystems — all handling real transactions from real users. When something breaks, there's no "works on my machine." It has to work for everyone.

The difference between a tutorial and production is that in production, your bug costs someone real money. That pressure taught me more than any course ever could. — Day and night debugging builds character

Outside of work, I mess around with cybersecurity — CTF competitions, Burp Suite, breaking things just to understand how they work. I hold a DROP Certified Security Course cert. Knowing how systems get attacked made me a paranoid — and better — developer.
